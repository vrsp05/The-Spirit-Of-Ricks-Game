The Spirit of Ricks · Quest 1 of 3 · as built on game-foundation

Q1: Hire the Professors

Ricks College is growing and needs new faculty. As President Bednar's assistant, the player brings four candidates to teaching demonstrations in real classrooms, and plays each demo as the candidate.

  • Fall 1999
  • 4 classroom demos
  • Easy / Hard
  • Keyboard/mouse + ESP32 controllers
  • Gives back 1/3 of the colour

Story and setting

  • When: Fall 1999, the world's starting era. The Kimball Building was dedicated that September, and the old Spori is in its final school year. Nothing in Q1 says "BYU–Idaho."
  • Who you are: President Bednar's assistant. You get the quest in his Kimball office and report back to finish it.
  • Where hiring happens: Personnel Services, a room off the Kimball hall. The Kimball really housed personnel offices.
  • Perspective: you walk as the assistant. In each classroom you become the candidate ("You are now the Math candidate"), then you're back to yourself.
  • The thread: the art studio's old overhead projector always shorts out during the art demo. Engineering repairs it.

Quest flow

  1. Bednar assigns the quest: four candidates are waiting in Personnel Services, and art comes before engineering.
  2. In Personnel Services (counter, chairs, hiring board) you pick up the first candidate. "The other candidates will meet you outside their buildings."
  3. The candidate walks beside you and says two walk-and-talk lines that set up their demo.
  4. Walk into their building's classroom and the walk-in plays, then the demo.
  5. Pass, and the chair gives the verdict and the candidate thanks you. The objective then points to the next candidate, already waiting outside their building, so there are no trips back to the Kimball.
  6. Suggested route: math, then English, then art, then engineering. Engineering stays locked until art is done.
  7. After all four, report to Bednar. Q1 is complete.

The classroom walk-in

Walking into a classroom starts this short scene automatically. Esc skips it, and later visits play only the last beat.

1 · ArrivalStudents turn to the door. "Is that the new math teacher?"
2 · The aisleThe candidate walks up the side aisle to the front.
3 · The problemThe chair stands and sets it in one line.
4 · HandoffThe title is chalked on the board, "You are now…", and the strip slides up.

Every demo follows the same arc: the chair's setup, you teach, a twist, the chair's verdict. On Hard, the chair says "One more question before I decide" and asks a follow-up first.

The classrooms

All four are modelled on a real lecture room:

  • boards spanning the front wall with a wood ledge, and a screen case above;
  • white two-person tables and gray chairs in rows, with a centre aisle;
  • gray window shades and gray carpet;
  • a drop ceiling with light panels and a hanging projector, shown during scenes and demos.

The work happens on the room's own surfaces. T (right stick ↑) switches between camera views: board, poster or wall, class. A slim strip at the bottom holds only what needs precision: answer choices, colours, a small mouse pad.

Romney Building · 3rd floor · Math & Computer Science

Math: "Binary, the Nephite way"

Chair: "Our computer science students keep tripping on binary — let's see if this helps."

In the roomThe chalkboard shows the student's question and the shum · seon · senine board, which lights up the answer (1 1 0 → 6). The Alma 11 gold-coin poster hangs on the side wall.
In the stripAnswer choices on Easy, or the coin tray on Hard. Feedback, then Next.
  • Tutorial first (not scored, once per session): "Pay 5". Put down a shum, then a senine, with each coin highlighted, and the board reads 1 0 1 → 5.
  • Questions: Pay 6 · read "011" · Pay 7 (one limnah!) · why is a limnah worth 7 · Zeezrom's six onties = 42 days of pay (Alma 11:22). Hard adds Pay 9 and Pay 13.
  • Misses allowed: 2 on Easy, 1 on Hard. On Hard, the right amount with too many coins counts as a miss.

Smith Building · English classroom

English: "Editing for accuracy"

Chair: "Brother Price has kindly volunteered his write-up of the Kimball Building dedication."

In the roomThe whole summary is projected on the whiteboard as one page. Move word by word, mark a mistake, and the fix is written above it in red ink: 1999, over 1899,. The Church News fact clipping is pinned to the bulletin board.
In the stripProgress, the current line as clickable words (for mouse), and the correction choices.
  • Mistakes: 3 on Easy, 6 on Hard. Facts include the 1999 date, Rexburg, Idaho, Ricks College, and the building named for President Kimball and dedicated by President Hinckley.
  • Wrong marks: unlimited on Easy, 2 on Hard. A hint highlights the sentence. Brother Price reacts: "…yeah, okay, that's fair."

Original Spori Building · 3rd floor · art studio

Art: "Drawing what you see"

Chair: "Today: the grid method — if that old projector cooperates."

In the roomYou paint directly on the studio's gridded board, with the brush cursor drawn on it. The projector beams the temple reference onto the side wall, then shorts out and the wall goes dark.
In the stripColours, mirror, fill, check, the match meter (Easy) and a small mouse pad.
  • The short-out: the projector always shorts out, at 90 seconds on Easy and 40 on Hard. If you finish first, it pops right at the end. Either way you finish from memory, and the chair sends it to the engineering lab.
  • Subject: the temple, framed as the candidate's "vision of a temple on the hill," with the team's recording of "High on the Mountain Top" playing (drop it in public/audio/).
  • Scoring and extras: pass at 60% on Easy, 85% on Hard, and the drawing counts anywhere on the board. The class guesses as you draw, the first mirror stroke gets an "ooh," and passing shows a voxel reveal.

Austin Building · electronics lab · unlocks after Art

Engineering: "Fix it live"

Physical Plant: "It shorted out in the middle of the art demo. The art department needs it back."

In the roomRotate wire tiles on a pegboard on the lab bench, right beside the broken projector. Its lamp lights, its fan spins, and the fuse box flashes on a short.
In the stripThe goal, lamp and fan status (stopped / slow / full), and a small mouse pad.
  • Levels: get the lamp to light · lamp and fan at full (parallel) · a switch for the fan · a dim power light with a slow fan and the lamp bright (Hard) · Find the short: the board starts sparking and you find the stray wire bridging + to − (Hard).

Rules for all four

  • Inputs: keyboard/mouse and controllers both work everywhere, with no typing.
  • Every demo has: a hint, skip, retry and Easy/Hard. Map markers always show the next stop.
  • Colour: it comes back in steps as candidates are hired. Progress is saved after every hire.
  • Real people: shown respectfully, with no invented quotes. Candidates and students are fictional.
