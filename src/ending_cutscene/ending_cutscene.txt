ENGINE NOTES

- Reuse the existing voxel avatars, Bednar's animated mouth, subtitle system,
  stadium geometry, camera interpolation, fades and full-color renderer.
- Build a small stage and torch bowl from boxes. Animate a few orange/yellow
  emissive blocks for the flame, with one nearby warm light.
- Represent the drones with one instanced group of glowing cubes. Move the
  same lights between two predefined point formations: 25 and BYUI. Basic
  bright materials are sufficient; a separate bloom effect is optional.
- Use a modest number of crowd figures, reusing the same meshes and a few
  offset wave animations. Dress Thor and the supporters with simple voxel
  helmet/beard/costume pieces.
- Stage and shoot only the part of the stadium needed for these views.
  Use a separate night-lit celebration setup so the outdoor gameplay lighting
  can be restored cleanly when the player chooses Keep exploring.
- Subtitles carry all dialogue. The scene works without spoken audio.
- Keep the stadium celebration at 30 seconds. Its crowd waves, flame motion
  and final drone formation overlap across shots. Append the STC epilogue.
- Render the ceremony to a texture on the STC screen. The final title already
  lives on that screen, so the reveal uses a continuous camera pullback.
- Reuse the same four presenter models, audience, render target and room on
  replay; retain preview save safety and normal game-state restoration.
- Launch once after the final I is placed and quest completion is recorded.
  The current example quest places all four letters together; the final
  placement animation can show B, Y, U, then I, with the I as the trigger.
- This draft makes the stadium celebration the main ending directly after
  the sign is completed. The existing STC travel prompt would be replaced
  when this ending is implemented.

HISTORICAL / ADAPTATION NOTES

At the 2026 event, his label would read Elder David A. Bednar. His two lines
in this draft are fictional game dialogue, not historical quotations.

Event coverage identifies the torch lighters as Elder and Sister Bednar and
President and Sister Meredith. The staging here deliberately focuses on
Bednar while acknowledging the other participants.

Thor's return is documented. The two accompanying Viking figures, their
movements, the exact scene order, and the two drone formations are simplified
creative choices for this game. Costume details can follow event photographs
when we build the scene.
